## Description (App Store + Google Play, max 4000)

PocketChef helps you cook with what you already have.

Point your camera at your ingredients and PocketChef detects them, then suggests
recipes you can make right now — no last-minute trip to the shops required. Every recipe
carries a real, licensed photograph of the finished dish.

**SCAN YOUR KITCHEN**
Snap what you have on the counter. PocketChef recognises the ingredients and matches
them to recipes, showing exactly what you have and what is still missing.

**COOK WHAT YOU HAVE**
Browse thousands of everyday recipes ranked by how well they match your pantry. Filter by
time, difficulty and cuisine, then follow hands-free cooking mode with built-in timers.

**NIGERIAN CUISINE, DONE PROPERLY**
A hand-built library of authentic Nigerian dishes — Jollof rice, Egusi, Efo Riro, Afang,
Banga, Ogbono, Akara, Moi Moi, Suya, pounded yam and more — each with the heritage story
behind it.

**PLAN YOUR WEEK**
Turn recipes into a weekly meal plan in seconds, then generate a tidy shopping list for
everything you are missing.

**PERSONAL TO YOU**
Set your diets, allergies and goals so suggestions fit the way you actually eat.

**PRIVACY BY DEFAULT**
Your data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted to authorized systems only.
You can export or permanently delete your account at any time from Account Settings.

Cook more of what you have. Waste less. Eat well — with PocketChef.
